# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Designers, developers, business owners, and digital creators looking for high-end, aesthetic, scannable QR codes with personalized logos, custom shapes, and color gradients.

## Product Purpose

QUARANA is a web application that transforms standard black-and-white QR codes into liquid glass visual artwork with customizable central logos, dot/eye patterns, CTA frames, and scannability validation, exporting high-res PNG (up to 4K), SVG vectors, and printable PDFs.

## Positioning

Combines liquid glassmorphism UI aesthetic, real-time WCAG scannability safety shield, WebGL shader backgrounds, and deep customization without compromising scanner camera readability.

## Operating Context

Runs 100% client-side in browser (desktop & mobile) with privacy safety (no server upload required for data or logos).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Input Types:** URL/Text, Wi-Fi configuration, vCard digital business cards, WhatsApp messaging pre-fills, Social Media handles.
- **Visual Engines:** Central logo overlay with auto ECC Level H (30% recovery), solid/gradient dot patterns, custom eye frame/ball geometry, CTA frames.
- **Real-Time Validation:** WCAG contrast ratio engine & ECC validator protecting against unreadable combinations.
- **Export Capabilities:** PNG (512px to 4K Ultra HD), SVG vector files, A4 printable PDF flyers, clipboard copy, native Web Share API.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** QUARANA Liquid QR Studio
- **Aesthetic:** Dark Obsidian & Light Frosted Crystal mode, liquid glassmorphism, terminal CLI developer console styling, warm modern typography (Plus Jakarta Sans & Space Grotesk).
- **Tone:** Premium, ultra-clean, tactile, privacy-first, developer-centric.

## Product Principles

1. **Scannability First:** Aesthetic customizations must never compromise smartphone camera scanner readability.
2. **Client-Side Privacy:** All data processing, image manipulation, and vector generation occur locally in the browser.
3. **Tactile Micro-Physics:** Every interaction feels responsive, weighted, and alive with spring physics and modern typography.
